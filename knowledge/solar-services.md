# Solar Services Knowledge

## Products
- Residential rooftop solar, on-grid, 1 kW to 10 kW.
- Commercial and industrial rooftop solar, any size.
- Solar water pumps for farms.
- Panels, inverters, mounting structures, cabling, earthing, net meters.
- Site survey, installation, DISCOM net meter application, subsidy filing help.
- Cleaning and maintenance, inverter service, panel replacement under warranty.

## Buyer intents that qualify
- Homeowner asking the cost of a solar system for their home.
- Homeowner asking about subsidy, savings or payback.
- Factory, warehouse, hotel or hospital asking about solar for their roof.
- Housing society or RWA asking about solar for common facilities.
- Farmer asking about solar pumps.
- Solar owner reporting damage, low output, high bill, subsidy stuck, vendor vanished or inverter fault.

## Intents that never qualify
- A solar seller or installer advertising their own service.
- A competitor arguing about brands or prices.
- A job seeker looking for solar work.
- A news post or government announcement with no buyer in it.
- Engagement bait, crypto, gambling or unrelated posts.

## Pricing signals (2026, indicative only, verify on the day)
- About Rs 55,000 to Rs 85,000 per kW installed for a complete on-grid home system.
- 3 kW: Rs 1.60 to 2.55 lakh before subsidy, about Rs 0.82 to 1.32 lakh after subsidy.
- 5 kW: Rs 2.50 to 4.25 lakh before subsidy, about Rs 1.72 to 3.47 lakh after subsidy.
- Subsidy is capped at Rs 78,000 and does not rise above 3 kW.
- Commercial buyers get no residential subsidy but can claim 40 percent accelerated depreciation and GST input credit.

## Pain points and the matching fix
- Monkey damage: GI wire mesh cage, raised mounting, cable sheathing, anti-monkey spikes.
- Hail damage: panel replacement under warranty, stronger mounting, insurance where available.
- Low output: cleaning, shading check, tilt correction, string and inverter check.
- High bill after install: generation check, net meter reading check, load vs system size review.
- Subsidy stuck: DISCOM status check, vendor registration check, document review.
- Vendor vanished: service takeover, inverter and panel health check.
- Roof leak: anchoring and waterproofing repair by a proper installer.

## Warranty
- Client replacement warranty: 5 years. Coverage UNCONFIRMED. Use the phrase "5-year replacement warranty" in general only, and ask the buyer to DM for exact terms, until the client confirms coverage in writing.
