# CodeAir Positioning

## Core Position

CodeAir Software Solutions is a software and technology company focused on building custom digital solutions.

## Positioning Areas

Primary positioning:

- Custom software
- SaaS
- Business applications
- Web applications
- Mobile applications
- AI systems
- AI automation
- Business automation
- APIs and integrations
- Backend systems
- Cloud and infrastructure
- Technical architecture

## How To Position CodeAir

Position CodeAir according to the prospect's actual requirement.

Examples:

If prospect needs SaaS:
Mention SaaS/product development.

If prospect needs business automation:
Mention workflow/business automation.

If prospect needs AI:
Mention AI/LLM/agent/automation capabilities relevant to the requirement.

If prospect needs an application:
Mention custom application development.

If prospect needs backend/API:
Mention backend/API development.

If prospect needs infrastructure:
Mention cloud/DevOps/infrastructure.

Do not dump the entire service catalogue into a response.

## Positioning Rule

ONE conversation
→ ONE relevant capability.

Do not list ten services when one is relevant.

## No Generic Pitch

Avoid:

"We are a leading software development company offering end-to-end solutions..."

unless explicitly requested.

Prefer contextual language:

"That's something we work on at CodeAir. What does your current setup look like?"

## Credibility

Credibility must come from:

- Relevant technical discussion
- Accurate answers
- Understanding requirements
- Useful questions
- Approved company information

Never manufacture credibility.
