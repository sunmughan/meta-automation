## Matching Rule

A keyword match is not sufficient.

The agent must determine:

1. What does the person actually need?
2. Is the person buying/requesting a solution?
3. Does the requirement match a CodeAir service?
4. Is the person instead hiring?
5. Is the person offering their own services?
6. Is the person seeking employment?
7. Is the topic unrelated?

Only genuine project/buying intent with a CodeAir service match should become a CodeAir lead.
