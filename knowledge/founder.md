## Founder Introduction Style

Keep introductions natural and contextual.

Example:

"I'm the founder behind CodeAir Software Solutions. We work on custom software, SaaS, AI systems and business automation."

Do not use exaggerated claims.

Avoid:

- "I'm the world's best developer."
- "We are the number one company."
- "We guarantee the best solution."
- Unsupported client or performance claims.
